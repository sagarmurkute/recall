# Recall — Product Vision & Philosophy

> **"You already have the answer. Recall finds it."**

---

## 1. Product Identity & Definition
**Recall** is an AI-powered personal digital memory and retrieval engine designed specifically for students. It ingests the messy, unstructured artifacts of student life—course syllabi, lecture slide PDFs, whiteboard screenshots, assignment rubrics, and notes—and allows users to ask questions in natural language, retrieving verified answers backed by exact source citations.

### What Recall Is NOT:
* ❌ **NOT a generic chatbot:** Recall does not conduct open-ended creative conversations or hallucinate general knowledge.
* ❌ **NOT a generic cloud drive:** Recall is an active retrieval engine, not a passive file locker.
* ❌ **NOT a conventional keyword search engine:** Recall understands multi-modal content and semantic intent rather than relying solely on exact keyword matches.
* ❌ **NOT a social network or productivity suite:** Recall does not do team collaboration, Kanban boards, or rich-text doc editing.

**The Central Product Experience:** *"Find information I already have."*

---

## 2. Core Problem & Mission
Students accumulate thousands of information fragments across screenshots, PDFs, documents, notes, messages, emails, and links. They often remember *that* they saw or received a piece of information (e.g. a late submission policy, a room number, an assignment due date) but cannot locate it when they need it most.

**Recall's Mission:** Eliminate the cognitive tax of information management for students by transforming passive digital clutter into an active, grounded, and verifiable personal memory.

---

## 3. Guiding Product Principles

### Principle 1: Grounded Truth Over Conversational Filler
When a student asks *"When is my DBMS assignment due?"*, they need the exact date, the grading penalty for lateness, and a 1-click link to the exact PDF page or screenshot.

### Principle 2: Zero Organizational Tax
Recall requires zero manual filing, tagging, or folder tree setup. The user drops screenshots, PDFs, or notes into Recall; automated ingestion and semantic indexing handle structure automatically.

### Principle 3: Clear Separation: Direct Source Fact vs. AI Synthesis
Recall enforces a transparent boundary between:
1. **Verified Direct Quotes:** Verbatim statements extracted directly from uploaded files.
2. **AI Synthesis & Summary:** Inferences or contextual summaries generated by Google Gemini.
3. **Missing Information:** Explicit refusal to invent answers when sources lack evidence.

### Principle 4: Multi-Modal First-Class Citizens
Screenshots, phone photos of whiteboards, and unstructured PDFs are treated as first-class searchable data objects.

### Principle 5: Privacy-First Tenant Isolation
All user files and indexed embeddings are isolated through PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) and private storage buckets.

## 4. Why Recall Must Exist

| Vector | Existing Solutions | The Recall Way |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Traditional Search (Drive, Windows Search)** | Matches exact keywords; fails on screenshots, unstructured PDFs, and semantic questions. | Semantic understanding + Vision OCR + Hybrid retrieval. |
| **Generic Chatbots (ChatGPT, Claude)** | Hallucinates generic advice; knows nothing about specific college syllabi or assignment rules. | Grounded strictly in the student's own private repository with verifiable citations. |
| **Note Apps (Notion, Obsidian)** | Demands high manual filing effort; files become dead attachments. | Zero-effort ingestion; files are indexed and connected automatically. |
